Method and data

Frost dates

The planner finds the longest spell of days above 0 °C in each year. The day before the spell is the last spring frost. The day after is the first autumn frost. The safe date is the 90th percentile across ten years.

Soil temperature

A first-order lag model follows daily mean air temperature. The model is calibrated against observed soil at 6 cm. Recent values are observed. Future values are estimates.

Limits

  • Frost varies across a suburb. A cold hollow can frost when the grid cell does not.
  • Soil temperature is an estimate, not a probe reading.
  • Crop windows are indicative. Local varieties and microclimates differ.
